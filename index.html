WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
We champion stories rooted in the Filipino experience — stories that resonate locally and travel globally. We're particularly interested in:
  • Untold regional and provincial stories
  • Contemporary Filipino social realities
  • Mythological or folk horror reinventions
  • Cross-cultural and diaspora narratives
  • Genre films with Filipino soul
  • Documentary subjects with cinematic potential
SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
  • Filipino citizenship or residency preferred
  • Original material only — no adaptations without rights
  • Scripts not required for initial submission
  • One active pitch per writer at a time
  • Response time: 3–4 weeks
  • Batch 3 deadline: June 30, 2025
DEVELOPMENT FUND
Selected pitches may receive development funding of up to ₱500,000 for script development, attached to a first-look deal with Mayon Pictures.
